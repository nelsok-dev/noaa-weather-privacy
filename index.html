Privacy Policy

NOAA Weather (Unofficial)

Effective date: May 18, 2026

Overview

NOAA Weather (Unofficial) is a free, independent app that displays publicly available weather data from the U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NOAA, the NWS, or any U.S. government agency.

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information is accessed, how it is used, and what is never collected or shared.

Information We Access

Location data — The app may request access to your device's location solely to display weather forecasts relevant to your current area. Location data is sent directly to the NWS public API (api.weather.gov) to retrieve forecast data. It is never stored, logged, or transmitted to any other server.

You can deny location permission at any time in your device settings. The app will continue to work for any manually entered location.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use cookies or persistent tracking technologies.
  • We do not store location history or search history on any server.

Third-Party Services

The app fetches weather data exclusively from the National Weather Service public API (api.weather.gov). This is a free government service; its own privacy practices are governed by NOAA and the U.S. federal government.

No other third-party services, SDKs, or APIs are used.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected by the app at all, it is safe for use by all ages.
