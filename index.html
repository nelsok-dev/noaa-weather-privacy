Privacy Policy

NOAA Weather (Unofficial)

Effective date: August 1, 2026

In short

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. The app has no server that stores anything about you — with one exception: if you turn on push notifications, a small alert relay we operate has to keep your device's notification token and the location(s) you want alerts for, because that is the only way to know which device to warn when the National Weather Service issues something for your area. Turning notifications off deletes that record.

Overview

NOAA Weather (Unofficial) is a free, independent app that displays publicly available weather data from the U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NOAA, the NWS, NASA, or any U.S. government agency.

This policy explains what information the app accesses, where it is sent, what is stored, and for how long.

Location Data

When your location is read

Location access is optional and always requested through the standard iOS permission prompt. If you grant it, the app reads your device's location periodically while you are using it — not only when you tap the location button. Specifically, it may read your position when the app starts or returns to the foreground, when it refreshes alerts, and to keep the home screen widget current. Readings are cached for about 20 minutes so the app is not continuously polling your position.

The app does not use background location. It cannot read your position while it is closed. Severe-weather alerts arrive by push notification, which does not require background location access.

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings. The app remains fully usable — you can search for any city or ZIP code instead.

Where your location is sent

Your coordinates are sent to the public data services listed under “Third-Party Services” below in order to look up the forecast, alerts, tides, marine conditions, and air quality for that spot. Those requests are made directly from your device. Apart from the alert relay described in the next section, we do not operate any server that receives or stores your location.

Push Notifications and the Alert Relay

Push notifications are opt-in. If you enable them, the app registers with the NOAA Alert Relay, a small server we operate at noaa-alert-relay.nelsok.workers.dev, hosted on Cloudflare Workers.

The relay periodically checks the NWS public alert feed and sends a push through Apple's APNs service when an active alert covers one of your locations. It does not receive your name, email address, contacts, device identifiers beyond the APNs token, or anything tied to a user account. It is never used for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Only your current locations are kept — no location history. Each update overwrites the previous values. Your record is deleted when you turn notifications off in the app (the app asks the relay to remove it), and automatically when Apple reports your device token as no longer valid, which happens when you uninstall the app or disable notifications at the system level.

Opt-out. Turn off the notifications switch in the app's Settings, or disable notifications in iOS Settings → NOAA Weather Unofficial → Notifications.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following is kept locally on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your saved locations and the currently active one
  • Your settings — units, quiet hours, severity filter, briefing time
  • Recent alert history and which alerts you have already seen
  • Cached weather and map data, so the app works briefly without a connection
  • The random installation identifier described above

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Home Screen Widget

The optional home screen and Lock Screen widgets read your saved location and unit preferences from a storage area shared with the app, and fetch forecast data directly from the National Weather Service API in order to refresh. The widget does not send anything to us and has no access to your location beyond what the app has already stored.

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking software of any kind.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account, sign-in, name, or email address.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no participation in Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework — the app collects no tracking data.
  • No advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No cookies or cross-app tracking technologies.
  • No location history, search history, or behavioural profile.
  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared for marketing purposes. Ever.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. It requires no account and collects no personal identifiers from users of any age.
